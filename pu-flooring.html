Overview

Polyurethane (PU) flooring is ideal for environments that require thermal shock resistance, chemical resistance, and high durability.

Dry or Cold Storage & Freezers

Specially designed to withstand extreme sub-zero temperatures while maintaining a seamless, hygienic, and slip-resistant surface perfect for cold chain facilities.

Hatcheries & Food Processing

Providing heavy-duty protection against constant moisture and organic acids, ensuring a sterile environment that meets strict food safety and sanitation standards.

Dressing Plants

High-impact flooring solutions built for intensive cleaning cycles and heavy traffic, offering long-term durability in demanding meat and poultry processing areas.
