Overview

Our Polyurethane (PU) flooring is a high-performance, seamless, and heavy-duty solution specifically engineered for industries requiring extreme durability, thermal shock resistance, and superior hygiene standards.

Cold & Dry Storage

Designed to withstand extreme temperature fluctuations from -40°C to 120°C. It prevents floor cracking in walk-in freezers and provides a slip-resistant surface for dry warehouse operations.

Hatchery

Provides a non-porous and anti-microbial surface essential for hatcheries. It is resistant to constant washdowns and prevents the growth of bacteria, ensuring a sterile environment for livestock production.

Dressing Plant

Highly resistant to organic acids, blood, and fats found in meat processing areas. Its seamless finish allows for easy high-pressure cleaning and meets strict food safety regulations (HACCP/FDA).
